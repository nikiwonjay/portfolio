主要功能Key Features

01 / IMPORT

简历导入Résumé Import

支持 TXT、DOCX 与文本型 PDF，手动选择中文或英文，解析后导入资料库，减少逐条录入Supports TXT, DOCX and text-based PDF files, with manual selection of Chinese or English, importing parsed information into the library to reduce entry of individual items

02 / ORGANIZE

中英文资料库Chinese and English Information Library

按教育、实习、项目等经历管理记录，支持搜索、复制、编辑和删除，方便找到要用的内容Organizes records by education, internships, projects and other experiences, supporting search, copy, editing and deletion to make the information you need easier to find

03 / EXTEND

自定义分组Custom Groups

新增证书、培训等分组，在分组中添加多条记录和字段，按自己的简历结构组织资料Add groups for certificates, training and other categories, with multiple records and fields in each group to organize information according to your résumé structure

04 / MATCH

规则与 AI 字段匹配Rule-Based and AI Field Matching

本地规则即时推荐填写内容，选择后可一键填入当前输入框，也可调用 AI 理解不同网申系统的字段名称与分组归属，返回已有候选和判断理由Local rules instantly recommend information to enter, which can be selected and filled into the current field with one click, AI can also interpret field names and group membership across different application systems and return existing candidates with reasons for its judgment

05 / INTERACT

控件辅助选择Assisted Control Selection

对可靠识别的日期与下拉控件辅助选择，包含 Phoenix 日历适配，未适配的控件使用复制或手动填写Assists with selections in reliably recognized date and dropdown controls, including support for Phoenix calendars, controls without support can be filled by copying or entering information manually

06 / TRANSLATE

按需翻译On-Demand Translation

通过按钮触发翻译，浏览器支持时可使用本地翻译，也可配置自己的云端 AI 服务Translation is triggered by a button, using local translation when supported by the browser, or a cloud AI service that you configure

项目亮点Project Highlights

AI 辅助字段匹配AI-Assisted Field Matching

利用 AI 结合字段标题、分组与经历归属进行匹配，适配不同网申系统，识别“奖励”“获奖名称”等不同叫法，减少仅靠关键词带来的误判Uses AI to match fields based on field titles, groups and the experiences they belong to, adapting to different application systems and recognizing variations such as “Award” and “Award Name” to reduce misjudgments from relying solely on keywords

自动翻译Automatic Translation

按需将中文资料自动翻译为英文，中英文内容分别保存，减少英文网申中的重复翻译与录入Automatically translates Chinese information into English on demand, storing Chinese and English content separately to reduce repeated translation and entry in English-language applications

随时新增和修改字段Add and Edit Fields at Any Time

根据不同网申要求新增、修改字段和自定义分组，按自己的经历组织资料，更新后可继续用于后续申请Add and edit fields and custom groups according to different application requirements, organize information around your own experiences and reuse updated information in subsequent applications

资料默认保存在本地，AI 默认关闭，需自行配置，控件兼容性因网站而异，插件不提交申请Information is stored locally by default, AI is disabled by default and requires your own configuration, control compatibility varies by website, the extension does not submit applications
